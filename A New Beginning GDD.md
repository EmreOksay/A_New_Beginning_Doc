# 1.Story & Character's

## 1.1 Story
- The story starts with a young man named Jacob (could be customized) and his sister who he cares for deeply. Jacob having to take care of his sister alone after their parents, the lord and lady of the city as well as former adventurers, disappeared in a raid of the nearby dungeon. He tries his best to keep the house and the city running even with the pressure of the frequent monster attacks, which seems to increase in the frequency day by day, as well as the strenuous problems of the city like the production of the food, housing and etc. Even though he is working till he has lost a lot of weight becoming weaker by the day, he decides to pick up his short sword, left by his father, to help defend the town and save his sister after he learns she suffers from an incurable disease. Jacob remembers the tales of the old which speaks of the rewards and the miracles the dungeons hold and decides to start training and become an adventurer himself for his sisters sake. But with the administrative work of the city left to him he wouldn't be able to handle it on the side, which prompts him to hire a helper with the last of his wealth. He starts to slowly recover health and journeys out into wild, fighting monsters, hunting, foraging, looting the monsters, chests and alike to earn money and keep his house going and slowly, he starts to become more and more competent. Now, easily keeping his house afloat as well as starting to rebuild the city with the resources he gathers and bolsters the defences for the city. And eventually, he dives into the dungeon his parents was lost in to come across a mysterious curse. He finds out that he has become unable to die but at the same time his soul gets more and more trapped into the dungeon, his existence slowly fading away from his home world. He tries to solve the mysteries of the dungeon while meeting new people and enemies, while being forgotten by his friends and family in the city. Trying to save his sister, his friends in the city and maybe the people trapped in the dungeon, he is stuck in a gut wrenching battle not for his life but for his existence as he is at risk of being forever forgotten.

# 2. Gameplay

## 2.1 Goal

### 2.1.1 Short Term Goal
- In the short term the player needs to focus on grinding materials they will need to progress the game to the next stage. They will need to gather various resources to upgrade the character itself and its arsenal. They will need to periodically and systematically enter the tower and progress through the floors to open new materials and reach the next stage of progression.
### 2.1.2 Long Term Goal
- The overall goal of the game is to find a cure for your sister and discover the mysteries of the tower. While looking for these the player will slowly but steadily start restoring the town into its former glory.

## 2.2 Gameplay
- The player will start with very simple tools at their disposal and from they they will need to start gathering various resources that will aide them on their journey. The very first resource they will be able to obtain is wood. For this they will need to venture out in to the monster infested woods. With this wood they can go to town and repair the blacksmith. From this blacksmith they will be able to obtain better tools to gather better materials more efficiently. After this they can obtain a pickaxe and go to the quarry to gather materials such as stone, coal, copper or iron to make better armor and weaponry. And with their new equipment they will enter the tower for the first time and progress through the floor's until they die. When they die for the first time and go to the blacksmith to upgrade their gear the blacksmith will treat them like it is the very first time they are visiting him. From this point on the player will continue throughout this cycle until they complete the tower entirely.

## 2.3 Core Mechanics 
 - Simple Combat
	 1. The combat of the game is basic 2d top down roguelike. It will be like the combination of Enter The Gungeon and Stardew Valley. The weapon options are very limited in the early game but little by little the player will be able to use all kinds of spells alongside with different kinds of arrows and close combat options. 
 - Profession System
	 1. The profession system is similer to Stardew Valley and Skyrim. There will be all kinds of different sets of Skill tree's for example woodcutting, foraging, combat, magic etc.. The player will get exp depending on their actions like if they cut a lot of trees they will get woodcutting exp but if they are in combat they will get combat exp. When a certain amount of exp threshold is reached the player will gain a skill point and then be able to use these skill points to open up new abilities. These abilities can add new mechanics or change existing ones. For example they can change how their dash works or they can get a parry ability. 
 - Crafting
	 1. The crafting will mostly be done through the various merchants that will be spread around town. The player will still have the option to craft some items like cooking or making their own arrows but if they wanna do more specialized or complex stuff they will need to go to the merchants. For example even if the player can create arrows to make the bow they will need to go to the blacksmiths weaponry section. There will also special events related to crafting like a legendary wizard can craft you a very overpowered tome but the wizard will be inside the tower invisible so if the player wants that tome they will need to look  for an invisible wizard inside to tower. 
 - Town Building
	 1. When the player first enters the town they will see that the town itself is on the brink of collapse but they can repair certain houses and buildings by doing so they will increase the towns appeal. The appeal is the mechanic that decides how many people will move in to your land. The buildings the player can repair are divided into 3 categories
		 1. Decorative Buildings: These are buildings such as wells, statues, night lamps and such. The only plus these buildings give are appeal and nothing else.
		 2. General Buildings: These are buildings such as houses and restaurants. These buildings will allow more and more people to come to your land to settle down. When they do the player can start gathering tax from these people to fund their journey.
		 3. Specialized Buildings: These are buildings such as blacksmith, herbalist's house and merchant's shop. These buildings will allow the player to upgrade and craft better gear or gather special resources that are hard to come by.
